# SpeakBack — Character Art Brief

**Client:** Uprise Studios / J M Games (Jamie) · **Date:** 10 October 2026 · **Version:** 1.0  
**Game:** SpeakBack, a mobile voice party game. Players hear a phrase backwards, copy it, and hear their attempt flipped forwards. Robot characters ("the bot family") host and react.

## 1. What we need

Final, production-ready 2D character art for a cast of friendly robots, built so they can be animated by swapping parts in Unity. Prototype images are supplied as **mood and silhouette references only**. Please redesign freely within this brief; the final designs must be your original work.

## 2. Style guide

- **Look:** clean, glossy cartoon robots with thick dark outlines (about 6–8 px at 1024 px tall), rounded "sticker" proportions (large head, compact body) and soft cel shading with one highlight. This matches most of the prototypes.
- **Consistency:** every character must look like they belong in the same world, standing side by side on one screen. The Cora reference (pastel storybook scene) and the textured, rusty, gritty prototypes are tone references only; render them in the clean house style.
- **Palette:** game UI is dark steel `#0F151D`/`#1A2330` with cyan `#22D3EE` as the action colour and orange `#FF7A1A` as the brand colour. Characters should read clearly against dark backgrounds. Each character has one signature accent colour.
- **Readability:** silhouettes recognisable at 96 px tall. Faces (eyes/visor/screen) carry the expressions.
- **Tone:** funny, warm, never menacing or gross. Characters may be cheeky; they are never cruel.

## 3. Hard rules

- **No real-person likeness.** No character may resemble a real, living or historical person, including signature hairstyles, facial hair, glasses or props associated with a famous person. (For example, the genius bot must not have Einstein-style hair, moustache or "E=mc²".)
- **No existing characters, logos or trademarks**, and no designs that echo a famous robot from film, TV or games.
- **Cultural respect:** characters inspired by a culture or profession (samurai, cowboy, footballer, pop performer) are affectionate archetypes, never caricatures. No exaggerated features, mock writing or stereotyped props. We'll review these together.
- **Rights:** full assignment of copyright in all final artwork and source files to Uprise Studios / J M Games, including for marketing and merchandise, written into the contract. Please confirm you will not use generative AI tools for final deliverables, or tell us which tools you use so we can check their commercial terms.

## 5. Per-character deliverables

For every character:
1. **Turnaround or reference sheet:** front (primary), 3/4 and side, with colour callouts and hex values.
2. **Layered source file** (PSD, or Krita/Affinity with PSD export) at **2048 px tall**, with separate named layers or groups: `body`, `head`, `face_base`, `eyes_*` (or `visor_*`), `mouth_*`, `brows_*` if any, `arm_L`, `arm_R`, `hand_L`, `hand_R`, `legs`, `accessory_*`, `shadow`. Pivot points marked for arms and head.
3. **Expression set** (counts above; 3 for others: idle, win, lose) as layer variants that share one body, so swapping eyes/mouth/visor changes the expression.
4. **Exports:** transparent PNG per expression at 1024 px and 512 px tall, same canvas size and same foot position for every expression, so they line up when swapped.
5. **Ground shadow** as a separate PNG.

## 6. Logo and app icon (separate item)

- **Logo:** keep the mirrored "BACK" idea (SPEAK / mirrored BACK). Clean the style to match the house look; orange brand colour on dark steel. Deliver as vector (SVG/AI) plus PNGs.
- **App icon:** a single bot face (likely Uprise) on dark steel, legible at 48 px. Deliver an Android adaptive icon (separate foreground/background layers at 432×432) and a 1024×1024 master.
- Test that both read clearly at small sizes.

2. Uprise full set → Cora full set (Wave 1).
3. Waves 2–4 as agreed.
