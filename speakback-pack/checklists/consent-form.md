## What we are doing

We are testing how well a game can tell whether someone has copied a backwards phrase. You'll hear short silly phrases played backwards, copy them, and the game will record your attempts.

## What we record and why

- Short recordings of your attempts (about 10–20 clips, a few seconds each).
- A made-up speaker code (e.g. S03), a broad accent region and a broad voice range (higher/middle/lower) **that you choose**, and the phone model used.
- We do **not** record your name, contact details or anything else about you with the audio.

The recordings are used only to test and improve the game's scoring, including checking that scores are fair across accents and voices. A small number of people (the organiser and up to five raters) will listen to them.

## What we will not do

- Publish, share or sell your recordings.
- Use them to identify you, to build a voice profile, or to train a voice clone.
- Use them in marketing or in the released game.

## Storage and deletion

Recordings are stored on the organiser's encrypted computer, not in the game's code repository or any cloud service unless listed here: ____________. They will be deleted by **__ / __ / ____** (no later than 12 months from today), or sooner if you ask.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time during the session, and you can ask for your recordings to be deleted at any time before the deletion date by quoting your consent reference, with no reason needed.
