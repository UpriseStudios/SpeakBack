# Fun-Test Script (HUMAN STEP F)

**Build:** Playtest APK from P13. **Participants:** adults (18+), new to the game, who were not in the bake-off if possible. No recordings are kept; the Playtest build holds only anonymous counts in memory.

## Solo test (5+ people, about 10 minutes each)

1. **Set-up:** "This is an early game. I'm testing the game, not you. Please think out loud." Hand over the phone. Don't explain the game.
2. **Guided round:** observe silently. Note any point where they hesitate or ask.
3. **After the guided round:** "That's the tutorial. You can stop or keep going, up to you." Then wait. **Record how many extra rounds they choose unprompted.** This is the key number.
4. **Observe** (tally on the sheet below): laughs, uses of Hear what you said and Compare, Retry vs Next, frustration, comments about scores.
5. **Questions afterwards:**
   - In one sentence, what do you do in this game?
   - Was anything confusing?
   - Did the scores feel fair? Was there a moment one felt wrong? *(Ask which phrase, what score, and what they expected.)*
   - What was the funniest moment?
   - Would you play this with friends?
6. Open the hidden stats screen (5 taps on the logo), copy the numbers, then press **Reset session** before the next person.

## Group test (1+ session, 3–5 people, about 15 minutes)

1. "Take turns. Pass the phone after each go. Highest score each round wins."
2. Use **Next player** between turns. Let it run; don't facilitate.
3. Note: laughter (rough count), whether people ask to hear someone's raw attempt, arguments about scores, requests to continue when you suggest stopping.
4. Ask the group the same questions as in the solo test, plus: "What would make this better with friends?"

## Gates this feeds (Phase 1 §18)

- **Solo:** 4 of 5 or more understand after one guided round, and 3 of 5 or more choose 3 or more extra rounds.
- **Group:** record laughter, raw/compare use and requests to continue.
- **Credibility:** every "unfair" example is investigated in P14.
